About This Tool

This app helps Australians understand projected life expectancy based on socio-economic and demographic factors.

It uses data from the Person Level Integrated Data Asset (PLIDA), accessed via the Australian Bureau of Statistics' DataLab. The life expectancies are computed based on the 2016-2017 census population with improvement factors of ALT2015-17 (125 year).

Disclaimer

This tool provides general statistical information about life expectancy for groups of people with the characteristics you select. It is not a prediction for any individual and does not take account of personal circumstances such as health or lifestyle. It is not financial, retirement, insurance, medical or legal advice and should not be the only basis for any decision. Estimates are based on 2016–2017 data and modelling assumptions, and carry statistical uncertainty. Before making decisions that depend on this information, consider speaking to a suitably qualified professional.
